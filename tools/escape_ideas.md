# Escape room ideas – Night Train difficulty (build from Fri 20 Nov)

Same rules as The Last Night Train: 4 rooms, 13–15 puzzles, three parallel trails into one meta lock, decoys, info spread across rooms, hints per trail. New setting every time.

## 3. The Lighthouse at Gull Rock (Cornwall, 1912 – storm night)
Keeper Abel Penhallow vanished; a ship is due past the reef at 02:00 and the lamp is dark. Rooms: keeper's cottage, oil store, lamp room, cliff-top radio hut.
- Tide table + ship's schedule → the hour the ship passes the reef (time lock on the oil store).
- Semaphore/flag chart + a child's drawing of flags → word for the radio hut padlock.
- Lens prism puzzle: rotate 4 lens panels so a beam path lands on the reef marker (light-path grid).
- Logbook with a forged page (ink colour + the weather column contradicts the barometer) → names the saboteur's boat.
- Morse by lamp: the lighthouse's own character ("2 flashes every 10 s") vs. a false one.
- Meta: three lighting rings (oil, wick, mantle) set to three numbers → lamp lights, ship saved.
Decoys: a smuggler's lantern, a second logbook, a wrong flag set.

## 4. After Hours at the Museum of Natural History (London, 1958)
Locked in after closing; a meteorite has been swapped for a fake and the night guard's rounds start at 23:00.
- Specimen drawers with Latin names → alphabetical order gives a drawer code.
- Dinosaur skeleton: bones numbered, one is a plaster copy (weight scale) – hides a key.
- Planetarium projector: set the date of a star map to reveal a constellation = a symbol.
- Guard's round sheet + clock-in station times → the 7-minute gap to slip through.
- Mineral UV lamp: invisible ink on a museum label.
- Meta: three display-case keys (Earth, Sky, Sea halls) open the curator's safe with the real meteorite.
Decoys: a gift-shop replica, an old exhibition plan, a curator's love letter.

## 5. The Alchemist's Attic (Prague, 1610)
Rudolf II's court; an alchemist's apprentice must finish a formula before the guards come at dawn.
- Astrological wheel: align planets by a horoscope written for the emperor.
- Scales and weights in old units (grains, scruples, drams) – convert to balance.
- Alchemical symbols table → substitution cipher on a recipe.
- Colour-mixing flasks (pour order) → a coloured flame code.
- Mirror writing in Leonardo style + a hand mirror.
- Meta: four elements seal (earth/water/air/fire) from four rooms → the Philosopher's Stone chest.
Decoys: a forged formula from a rival, a cursed (harmless) amulet, an empty chest.

Art per room: 4 scenes 1600×900 + optional close-ups, same brief format as briefs-escape-rooms.txt.
