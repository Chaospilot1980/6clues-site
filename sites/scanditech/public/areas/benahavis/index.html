Home automation in Benahavís.

Benahavís is where many of the largest homes on the coast sit: hillside estates, several buildings on one plot, long distances between the gate, the main house and the pool house. That is exactly the kind of project where a well-designed KNX system pays for itself, and where a poorly documented one becomes a problem.

Large plots, one system

Main house, guest house, staff quarters, gatehouse and garden lighting on one KNX installation, with sensible line and area topology so the system stays fast and easy to extend.

Run from a distance

Many owners in La Zagaleta, El Madroñal and Los Arqueros are away for months at a time. Holiday mode, leak protection, presence simulation and secure remote access keep the house looked after.

Climate on a hillside

Sun on one façade, wind on another. Room-by-room climate, automated shading and weather-station logic keep a large villa comfortable without wasting energy.

Audits and takeovers

Bought a villa with an undocumented system? We audit, document and take over existing KNX installations, including ones where the original installer has disappeared.

02 · Discretion

Your home stays yours.

Many of our clients would rather nobody knew what their house can do, or who lives in it. We treat that as a matter of honour, not a line in a policy.

Discreet by default
  1. No names, no addressesWe never publish who we work for or where. Not on our website, not on social media, not in conversation with other clients.
  2. No photos of your homeWhat we show is hardware and craft: boards, keypads, details. Never your house, your garden or your view.
  3. Confidentiality in writingWe are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before the first site visit.
  4. Your data stays in the houseThe system runs locally. No cloud account in our name, and remote support only when you allow it.
  5. Documentation belongs to youThe ETS project, drawings and access codes are handed over to you, not held back.

Questions we are often asked

Do you work in La Zagaleta and El Madroñal?

Yes. We work on villas throughout Benahavís, including gated communities such as La Zagaleta, El Madroñal, Los Arqueros and Monte Mayor, and we coordinate access with security and estate management.

How far is Benahavís from your office?

Our office is in central Marbella, roughly 20–30 minutes away, so site visits and service calls are quick.

Can you work alongside our architect and builder?

Yes. On new builds and major renovations we join early, specify the KNX system with the architect and electrician, and then programme and commission it ourselves.

Is everything kept confidential?

Always. We do not publish client names, addresses or photos of your home, and we are happy to sign an NDA before the first visit.
