Home automation in Sotogrande.

Sotogrande is quieter than Marbella and the homes are often larger: wide plots, separate guest houses, staff quarters and owners who spend part of the year elsewhere. A KNX system here has to run for months on its own – and has to be documented well enough that the next technician understands it.

Second homes that look after themselves

Holiday mode, leak protection, frost and humidity control, presence simulation and secure remote access – so the house is ready when you arrive and safe while you are away.

Large plots, one system

Main house, guest house, staff quarters, gatehouse and garden on one KNX installation with a clean topology, so it stays fast and easy to extend.

Golf, polo and sea views

Automated shading and lighting scenes that follow the sun, and climate that adapts to an open-plan house facing the course or the sea.

Takeovers and audits

Many homes in Sotogrande have KNX systems installed years ago. We audit, document and take over existing installations, including undocumented ones.

02 · Discretion

Your home stays yours.

Many of our clients would rather nobody knew what their house can do, or who lives in it. We treat that as a matter of honour, not a line in a policy.

Discreet by default
  1. No names, no addressesWe never publish who we work for or where. Not on our website, not on social media, not in conversation with other clients.
  2. No photos of your homeWhat we show is hardware and craft: boards, keypads, details. Never your house, your garden or your view.
  3. Confidentiality in writingWe are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before the first site visit.
  4. Your data stays in the houseThe system runs locally. No cloud account in our name, and remote support only when you allow it.
  5. Documentation belongs to youThe ETS project, drawings and access codes are handed over to you, not held back.

Questions we are often asked

Do you work in Sotogrande?

Yes. We work throughout Sotogrande – Sotogrande Alto, La Reserva, Valderrama, Almenara and the Marina – and the surrounding area.

Can you look after a house we only use part of the year?

Yes. We set up remote monitoring and alarms (with your permission) and can visit for service and seasonal checks.

Can you take over a KNX system another installer put in?

Yes. We audit the installation, recover or rebuild the ETS project and document everything before making changes.

Is everything kept confidential?

Always. We never publish client names, addresses or photos of your home, and we sign an NDA on request.
