Home automation in Estepona.

Estepona and the New Golden Mile are where much of the coast is being built right now: new villas, frontline apartments and complete developments. The best moment to plan a KNX system is before the walls are closed – and the second best is when you take over a home whose system nobody has documented.

New builds, planned properly

We join architects and electricians at drawing stage, specify the KNX system and cabling, and programme and commission it ourselves – so the house works the day you move in.

Apartments and penthouses

Compact KNX systems for lighting scenes, climate per room, motorised blinds and a single calm keypad per room – designed to fit community rules and existing wiring.

Sea-front climate

Salt air, strong sun and wind from the sea. Automated shading, weather-station logic and room-by-room climate keep a frontline home comfortable and efficient.

Developer support

Independent KNX specification and quality checks for developers in Estepona who want a system that buyers can actually use and maintain.

02 · Discretion

Your home stays yours.

Many of our clients would rather nobody knew what their house can do, or who lives in it. We treat that as a matter of honour, not a line in a policy.

Discreet by default
  1. No names, no addressesWe never publish who we work for or where. Not on our website, not on social media, not in conversation with other clients.
  2. No photos of your homeWhat we show is hardware and craft: boards, keypads, details. Never your house, your garden or your view.
  3. Confidentiality in writingWe are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before the first site visit.
  4. Your data stays in the houseThe system runs locally. No cloud account in our name, and remote support only when you allow it.
  5. Documentation belongs to youThe ETS project, drawings and access codes are handed over to you, not held back.

Questions we are often asked

Do you work in Estepona and the New Golden Mile?

Yes. We work throughout Estepona, the New Golden Mile and Casares, on new builds, renovations and existing KNX systems.

Can KNX be added to a new-build apartment?

Usually yes. The earlier we are involved, the better – ideally before the electrical installation is finished – but compact systems can also be added to existing apartments.

Do you work for developers?

Yes. We specify KNX systems, review installers' work and commission systems for small and mid-size developments.

Is everything kept confidential?

Always. We never publish client names, addresses or photos of your home, and we sign an NDA on request.
