01 · Our approach

Scandinavian precision, Mediterranean living.

Automation goes wrong when it is sold as gadgets. We start from the plans and the way the house is actually used — where you walk in at night, which terrace takes the afternoon sun, how the pool and the guest wing are managed when nobody is here.

Then we build it on KNX: an open, vendor-neutral standard with three decades of installed base behind it. Nothing depends on one manufacturer staying in business, and every project leaves our hands fully documented.

04 · Discretion

Your home stays yours.

Many of our clients would rather nobody knew what their house can do, or who lives in it. We treat that as a matter of honour, not a line in a policy.

Discreet by default
  1. No names, no addressesWe never publish who we work for or where. Not on our website, not on social media, not in conversation with other clients.
  2. No photos of your homeWhat we show is hardware and craft: boards, keypads, details. Never your house, your garden or your view.
  3. Confidentiality in writingWe are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before the first site visit.
  4. Your data stays in the houseThe system runs locally. No cloud account in our name, and remote support only when you allow it.
  5. Documentation belongs to youThe ETS project, drawings and access codes are handed over to you, not held back.

Questions we are often asked

What is KNX?

KNX is an open, worldwide standard for home and building control (ISO/IEC 14543), supported by around 500 manufacturers. Lighting, climate, shading, security and energy all communicate on the same bus, so you are never tied to one brand.

Do you work outside Marbella?

Yes. We are based on Avenida Ricardo Soriano in Marbella and work along the Costa del Sol — from Sotogrande and Estepona through Benahavís, Nueva Andalucía and San Pedro to Fuengirola and Málaga.

Can you help if my house already has a KNX system?

Yes. We audit and take over existing installations, recover or rebuild the ETS project and document the system — including installations where the original installer is no longer available.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Swedish and Danish, with Spanish on site with electricians and builders.

How much does a KNX system cost?

It depends on the size of the house and what you want to control. After a consultation we deliver a fixed scope and load schedule your electrician can price against, so you know the cost before work starts.
