Xcover

The walkable pool cover. Terrace by day, pool in seconds.

The idea

A pool occupies the best part of the plot, and for most of the year nobody is in it.

Xcover is a load-bearing, motorised covering that sits flush with the surrounding terrace. Closed, it reads as floor: you can put a table on it, let the children run across it, host on it. Open, the pool is simply there.

Because we build automation for a living, control is not an afterthought. Xcover is designed to sit on the house's KNX bus alongside lighting, shading and climate — with its safety logic in hardware, not in an app.

What it is for

Usable square metres

Closed, the cover is terrace. A pool that is out of use for most of the year becomes floor you can furnish, dine on and host on.

Safety by default

A load-bearing surface rather than a floating sheet: no gap to slip through for children, guests or animals.

Heat and water kept in

A sealed cover holds warmth overnight and cuts evaporation — less heating, less refilling, fewer chemicals.

Clean and quiet

Leaves, dust and Saharan calima stay out of the water, so filtration runs shorter and the pool is ready when you are.

Part of the house

Designed for KNX: one command puts the terrace, its lighting and the cover into the same evening scene.

Architect-led detailing

Flush edge profiles and material choices intended to read as continuous terrace, not as pool equipment.
