Motorised blinds, awnings and pergolas with KNX

Sun-tracking, wind and rain protection, and glare control tuned per façade. Terraces on the Costa del Sol live or die on this: get it right and the house cools itself, get it wrong and nothing else in the system can save it.

  • Sun tracking
  • Wind & rain safety
  • Per-façade logic
  • Pergolas

Shading that follows the sun

We programme blinds, screens and louvres per façade using the sun's position through the day and the year. Rooms stay bright without glare, and the heat stays outside.

Protection from wind and rain

A weather station on the roof retracts awnings and opens pergola louvres when the wind picks up, and closes them when it rains. Safety functions run on the KNX bus itself, so they work even if the internet is down.

Terraces and pergolas

Bioclimatic pergolas, awnings and outdoor screens are tied into the same scenes as the lighting and heating, so an evening on the terrace is one button, not five remote controls.

Questions we are often asked

Which blind motors do you work with?

Most professional motors from Somfy, Becker and others can be integrated via KNX actuators or KNX interfaces.

Will the awnings retract automatically in strong wind?

Yes. Wind and rain safety is handled by the KNX weather station and has priority over all other commands.
