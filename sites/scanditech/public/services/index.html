Services

KNX home automation services — one system for the whole house.

We work as the automation partner on new villas and renovations in Marbella and along the Costa del Sol, and as an independent consultant for architects, developers and other installers who need a second set of eyes on a KNX specification. Working in Benahavís or Estepona? See our area pages.

01

KNX Design & Programming

We take the architect's plans and produce the bus topology, panel layout, load schedule and ETS project behind them — then commission on site and hand over a documented system, not a black box. New builds and staged renovations alike.

  • Panel design
  • As-built documentation
  • Commissioning

Technologies

We commit to a small set of components we trust, and know them properly.

Everything below is open KNX or runs locally in the house — no proprietary bus, no subscription, no single supplier who can strand you. It is also, frankly, better looking than what comes as standard on a Spanish new-build.

01Visualisation & interface

Home Assistant

The visualisation layer on top of KNX: floor plans, dashboards and remote access on phone, tablet and wall panel. It runs locally in the house, so the system keeps working when the internet does not — and it lets us bring in what is not KNX (media, EV chargers, inverters, appliances) without compromising the bus.

home-assistant.io ↗
02Electrical board

Hager · ABB

The board is where a KNX installation is won or lost. We build with Hager or ABB actuators, dimmers and power supplies — European industrial hardware with long product lifecycles and spares you can still buy in ten years. Generously sized, clearly labelled, and documented so any electrician can work in it after us.

hager.com ↗abb.com ↗
03Sensors & touch controls

Tense · Basalte

What you actually touch. Tense and Basalte KNX keypads, thermostats and presence sensors in glass, steel, brass and stone — a long way from the white plastic rocker that comes as standard here. Chosen with the architect so the wall plates belong to the interior instead of interrupting it, and native KNX, so nothing depends on a proprietary protocol.
