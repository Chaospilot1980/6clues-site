About

A Scandinavian technical practice, based in Marbella.

Scandinavian Technical Solutions S.L. is a small, deliberately hands-on company on Avenida Ricardo Soriano. We take on a limited number of projects a year so that the person who designs your system is the person who commissions it — and the person who picks up the phone two years later.

Northern European standards of documentation and follow-up, Spanish site reality, and no interest in selling you more hardware than the house needs.

Open systems only

KNX and other open standards. Your house should not depend on one manufacturer's cloud, or on us being the only ones who can maintain it.

Documented, always

Every project leaves with as-built drawings and an ETS project file. If you ever replace us, the next engineer will thank you.

Small on purpose

A handful of projects a year. Fewer clients, more attention, and a phone number that still works after handover.

Where we work

Our office is in central Marbella. We work on villas and developments along the Costa del Sol — Marbella, Nueva Andalucía, the Golden Mile, Benahavís, La Zagaleta, Estepona, San Pedro de Alcántara, Sotogrande, Mijas, Fuengirola and Málaga.

Many of our clients are from Scandinavia and Northern Europe and spend part of the year elsewhere. We speak English, Swedish and Danish with owners, and Spanish on site.
