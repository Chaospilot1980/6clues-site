# Six Clues – escape room scenes (11 images)

Each image: 1600 × 900 px (16:9), JPG under 600 KB. Painted, cinematic noir illustration, same style as the Six Clues portraits. No people, no readable text, no logos. Objects MUST sit where described (left/centre/right, top/bottom) – players click on those areas. Leave the bottom 25 % of the image as floor, quieter in detail.
ZIP folders: the-last-night-train/ and the-clockmakers-last-hour/

## the-last-night-train/compartment.jpg
Art-deco sleeping-car compartment on the Orient Express, 1934, at night, warm lamp light, polished walnut panels. Top-left corner: a small brass door plaque. Left edge: a dark overcoat on a hook. Top centre: a window with snow streaming past, dark hills. Top right: a brass luggage rack with a round red hatbox. Centre-left, lower: a made-up berth in deep red velvet, a paperback lying on it. Centre-right, lower: a leather briefcase with three small brass dials. Far right: the open sliding door to the corridor.

## the-last-night-train/dining.jpg
Orient Express dining car at night, white tablecloths, art-deco lamps, three windows along the top with snow. Left wall: a framed railway timetable (unreadable). Next to it, left-centre: a framed route poster with a dotted route and town crests. Centre: one laid table with a silver sugar bowl and a red flower. Right-centre: a steward's wooden lectern with a list. Far right top: a conductor's navy jacket on a hook. Far right edge: a narrow door to the next car with a brass combination lock.

## the-last-night-train/van.jpg  (NEW VERSION – replaces the first one)
Mail and luggage van of a 1930s train, dim, a single hanging lamp, wooden ribs. Far left: the open doorway back to the lit dining car. Left half: a stack of five suitcases and trunks with paper luggage tags. Centre: a small desk under a window with a brass telegraph key and paper tape curling from the receiver. Right of the desk, low on the floor: a small grey postal strongbox. FAR RIGHT, full height: the end wall of the van with a narrow, riveted IRON DOOR with a small porthole and a square keyhole – clearly a door to the coal tender, NOT a safe or cabinet. No people, no text.

## the-last-night-train/engine.jpg
Cab of a 1930s steam locomotive at night. Centre: the open firebox glowing orange. Top left and top right: round brass pressure gauges. Left: a small chalk slate hung on the wall. Right: a bank of five brass levers with red knobs over a steel panel. Bottom left: the way back to the tender.

## the-clockmakers-last-hour/workshop.jpg
Copenhagen clockmaker's attic workshop, New Year's Eve 1931, snow on the window, warm lamp light. Top-left: a wall with five round clocks (red, blue, green, yellow rims and one plain). Top centre: small attic window with moon and snow. Centre-right top: a carved cuckoo clock. Right of it: a dark coat on a hook. Centre-bottom: a long workbench with a drawer (four-number lock), a small brass maker's plate and a letter. Far right: a closed wooden door.

## the-clockmakers-last-hour/study.jpg
The clockmaker's study: left, a tall bookshelf; left-centre, a globe on a stand; centre, a gramophone with a brass horn on a cabinet; right-centre top, a wall safe; right-centre bottom, a heavy padlocked chest; far right, a narrow door. Green walls, red rug, lamp light.

## the-clockmakers-last-hour/tower.jpg
Inside a clock tower at night: the back of a huge translucent clock face fills the centre top; left, a long pendulum rod with an empty hook; centre-bottom, an iron gear train with three empty pegs; right, a small brass door with a heart engraved on it. Moonlight through the clock face, dust in the air.

## the-lighthouse-at-gull-rock/cottage.jpg
Keeper's cottage at the foot of a Cornish lighthouse, storm night 1912, lamp light, whitewashed stone. Far left: a yellow oilskin coat on a hook. Left-centre top: a framed tide table. Top centre: a small rain-lashed window. Centre-bottom: a desk with a logbook, a letter and a child's crayon drawing of flags. Right of centre top: a tall wooden barometer. Right-centre bottom: a red hand lantern on the floor. Far right top: an iron door to the tower stairs. Far right bottom: a plank door to the oil store. Bottom left: the way out to a cliff path.

## the-lighthouse-at-gull-rock/oil.jpg
Stone oil store: left half three green oil drums; centre a brass paraffin tank with a tap and a red oil can; right top a printed chart of international signal flags pinned to the wall. Single lantern, dark corners. Exit bottom left.

## the-lighthouse-at-gull-rock/radio.jpg
Wooden radio hut on a cliff, storm outside. Centre-left: an early wireless set on a desk with brass dials and a small drawer. Centre-right: a printed list pinned to the wall. Right top: a rain-streaked window with lightning over the sea. Exit bottom left.

## the-lighthouse-at-gull-rock/lamp.jpg
Lighthouse lamp room at night, glass gallery all round, storm outside. Centre: a big Fresnel lens made of four square glass panels. Left: brass clockwork for the rotating shutter. Right: the paraffin burner with its glass chimney. Stairs down at bottom left.
