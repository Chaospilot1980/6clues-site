## the-last-night-train/compartment.jpg
Art-deco sleeping-car compartment on the Orient Express, 1934, at night, warm lamp light, polished walnut panels. Top-left corner: a small brass door plaque. Left edge: a dark overcoat on a hook. Top centre: a window with snow streaming past, dark hills. Top right: a brass luggage rack with a round red hatbox. Centre-left, lower: a made-up berth in deep red velvet, a paperback lying on it. Centre-right, lower: a leather briefcase with three small brass dials. Far right: the open sliding door to the corridor.

## the-last-night-train/dining.jpg
Orient Express dining car at night, white tablecloths, art-deco lamps, three windows along the top with snow. Left wall: a framed railway timetable (unreadable). Next to it, left-centre: a framed route poster with a dotted route and town crests. Centre: one laid table with a silver sugar bowl and a red flower. Right-centre: a steward's wooden lectern with a list. Far right top: a conductor's navy jacket on a hook. Far right edge: a narrow door to the next car with a brass combination lock.

## the-last-night-train/van-v2.jpg  (NEW VERSION – file name van-v2.jpg)
